    To view in 3D, download the MAProgress file and open it in Google Earth by selecting it from your notifications area.

    For full playback use this file.


    1. Go to earth.google.com/web
    2. Click on the Projects icon
    3. Click on the Open/New project button
    4. Click on Import KML file from computer
    5. Select (.kmz) file from your downloads area
    6. Click on the right arrow beside Routes to expand it
    7. Double-click on the relevant route to zoom in to fit the route on the screen
    8. Scroll down the menu and click on a team to zoom in
    1. If you do not have Google Earth installed on your computer, Download and install Google Earth first.
